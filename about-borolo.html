About Us

Exploring the depths

We are Group 15 members , A team of passionate student at the University of FORT HARE dedicated to sharing wonder of marine science through digital storytelling. Our goal is to highlight the impotence of ocean conservation and the incredible biodiversity found beneath the waves.

The Blue Frontier

Marine Biology and Oceon Life is a vast field that explores everything from the microscopic organisms that produce our oxygen to the massive mammals that migrate across globe-spanning currents. This theme focuses on the dilicate balance of aquatic ecosystems and the agent need to protect our Earth's blue heart.

  • The importance of coral reef biodivesirty.
  • Impact of climate change on ocean temperatures.
  • The role of phytoplankton in carbon sequestration.
  • Conservation strategies from endangered marine species.
